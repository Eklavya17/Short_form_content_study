Welcome

This study compares experiences of watching short-video feeds. You will rate your topic interests, watch videos in three parts, and answer three brief questions after each part. You can watch each video as long as you like, skip ahead, and optionally like videos.

We save the following under your participant ID:

  • Your consent and its time, topic-interest ratings, survey answers, and submission times.
  • The videos and feed order shown, viewing time, swipe positions, likes, replays, pauses, buffering, playback errors, and when the study tab becomes hidden or visible.
  • Browser/device information reported by your browser, event timestamps, and the feed's video-selection decisions.

The study does not record your camera or microphone. Participation is voluntary: you can stop at any time by closing the page. Data already submitted will remain saved.
